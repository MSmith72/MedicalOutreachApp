01 — Impact Report

Our Impact

What we've accomplished

From assembling supplies to briefing state leadership, here's the work behind the mission so far.

Title I Outreach

Leading a youth program that aims to provide essential emergency response and prevention training directly to Title I schools across Colorado.

Medical Drives

Ran community-wide medical drives and fundraisers to gather supplies. We successfully assembled and distributed 50 emergency medical bags.

Student Education

Educated the next generation by leading a comprehensive presentation for 150 students and staff at Northglenn Middle School.

State-Level Advocacy

Presented our program's mission, goals, and community impact directly to the Colorado Attorney General.
